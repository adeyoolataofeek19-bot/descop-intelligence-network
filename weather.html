WEATHER KNOWLEDGE

Know what the forecast means.

Quick explanations for interpreting weather conditions.

01

Temperature

The temperature describes how warm or cold the air is. The feels-like value also considers other environmental factors.

02

Humidity

Relative humidity measures how much moisture the air contains compared with the maximum it could hold at that temperature.

03

Rain probability

A precipitation probability indicates the chance of precipitation during the forecast period, not the percentage of the day it will rain.

04

Wind speed

Wind speed describes how quickly air moves. Strong winds can affect travel, outdoor activities and local conditions.

PREPAREDNESS

Weather safety.

Heavy Rain

Stay alert during intense rainfall

Monitor local forecasts, avoid flooded roads and never walk or drive through fast-moving floodwater.

Extreme Heat

Protect yourself from high temperatures

Drink water regularly, limit strenuous outdoor activities during peak heat and seek shade when possible.

Thunderstorms

Take precautions during storms

Move indoors when thunder approaches and avoid exposed areas, isolated trees and open fields.
